Regularization path

x is spread uniformly on [−1, 1]. Polynomials are fit in the Legendre basis (the same curves as 1, x, x², …, just numerically stable) by least squares with Householder QR. Ridge minimizes (1/n)Σ(y − ŷ)² + λΣβⱼ² on standardized features. LASSO minimizes (1/2n)Σ(y − ŷ)² + λΣ|βⱼ| by coordinate descent with soft-thresholding, which is why its coefficients can be exactly zero. The intercept is never penalized. Validation uses 300 fresh points from the same curve.
